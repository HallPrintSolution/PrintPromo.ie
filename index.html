About PrintPromo.ie

Located in Tallaght, Dublin, PrintPromo.ie is a customer-focused promotional print company, specializing in high-quality swag for every event.

Unlike many promotional companies, PrintPromo.ie boasts its own industrial and versatile in-house, multi-award-winning print department.

We take pride in offering innovative print solutions and are committed to excellence, providing urgent and bespoke print finishes for both trade clients and end customers.

Our Processes

At PrintPromo.ie, we specialize in printing both flat and 3D objects using a variety of techniques, including:

  • Industrial screen printing
  • Rotary printing
  • Pad Printing
  • Laser engraving
  • DTF transfer
  • Large format printing
  • Giclee printing
  • Hand printing or machine printing

Specialised ink finishes: We offer a wide range of specialized ink finishes, such as: Fluorescent inks, Phosphorescent inks, Metallic inks, Pure Pantone inks and Spot colours

Printing: We print on a wide variety of materials, including: Wood, Metal, Powder-coated products, Concrete, Fabric, Paper, Tissue, Plastics, Leather, Glass

Our in-house graphic design studio, combined with cutting-edge large-format printing and finishing equipment, allows us to meet the highest standards for both local and international clients. We proudly serve many of Ireland's trade printers and prestigious corporate brands, delivering detailed, technical finishes across a wide range of products.

No matter the size, complexity, or urgency of your project, we're ready to take it on. Speak to us about your printing needs, and we'll deliver exceptional results with reliability and precision.

Bleed

A term used in printing that refers to the area beyond the edge of the final printed page.

×

CMYK

CMYK is a color model used in color printing that stands for Cyan, Magenta, Yellow, and Key (black).

×

Vector File

A vector file is a type of graphic that uses mathematical equations to create shapes, lines, and colors. This allows the image to be scaled infinitely without losing quality.

×

High Resolution

High resolution refers to images or displays with a large number of pixels, resulting in finer detail and clarity.

×

Screen Print

Screen printing is a method of printing where ink is pushed through a screen or mesh stencil onto a surface, creating designs or images.

×

Pantone

Pantone is a standardized color matching system used in printing and design to ensure consistency of colors across different materials and devices.

×

Digital Print

Digital print refers to printing directly from a digital file, allowing for quick and cost-effective production of materials like photos and documents.

×

Plotting

Plotting involves creating precise graphics and designs using plotter machines, often for technical drawings or large-scale prints.

×

Halftone

Halftone is a technique that simulates continuous-tone images by using dots of varying size and spacing to create the illusion of gradient tones.

×

Litho Print

Litho print, or lithography, is a printing process where an image is created on a flat surface and ink is applied to the image area, transferring it to paper or other materials.

×

Pad Print

Pad printing is a process where ink is transferred from a plate to a silicone pad and then onto the printing surface, suitable for printing on irregular shapes.

×

Digital Transfer

Digital transfer is a method where designs are printed onto transfer paper and then applied to various surfaces, often used for customizing apparel and products.

×

Embroidery

Embroidery is the craft of decorating fabric using needle and thread to create patterns or designs, commonly used on clothing and accessories.

×

Full-Colour

Full-colour printing uses a wide range of colors to reproduce images and designs in a wide spectrum, typically involving the CMYK color model.

×

Overprint

Overprint is a technique where one color is printed over another, often used to create rich colors or special effects without leaving gaps between the different ink layers.

×

Die Cutting

Die cutting is the process of cutting shapes from printed material using a sharp steel blade to create custom designs, such as labels or packaging.

×

Spot UV

Spot UV is a finishing technique where a glossy UV coating is applied to specific areas of a printed piece to create visual contrast and enhance its appearance.

×

Embossing

Embossing is a technique that presses a raised design into the material, creating a three-dimensional effect on the surface of printed materials for a tactile and visual impact.
